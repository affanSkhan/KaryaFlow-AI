# 90-second demo script

1. Open KaryaFlow AI and click **Launch demo**.
2. Three documents appear: PO-1042, invoice INV-8831, and delivery challan DC-3304.
3. The workspace shows four checks. Vendor, PO reference, and price reconcile.
4. Quantity is flagged: PO = 100, invoice = 120, delivery = 100.
5. Click **Evidence** to show the source snippet behind the field.
6. KaryaFlow recommends **Ask Vendor** and drafts a precise clarification.
7. Approve the action through the human approval gate.
8. Open **Audit trail** and show case creation, document uploads, analysis, action creation, and human approval.
