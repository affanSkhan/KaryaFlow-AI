## Before pressing Submit

1. Open the live demo in an incognito/private window.
2. Run **Launch demo** from the first screen.
3. Verify the mismatch is visible.
4. Open **Evidence** and show all three source values.
5. Review and approve **Ask Vendor**.
6. Open **Audit trail** and verify human approval is recorded.
7. Open the GitHub repository in a private/incognito window and confirm the README, docs, and sample data are publicly accessible.
8. Confirm the PPT opens without missing fonts/images.
9. Upload the demo video and check that the first 10 seconds clearly establish the problem and product.
10. Submit through the Unstop Round 2 submission form before the published deadline.
