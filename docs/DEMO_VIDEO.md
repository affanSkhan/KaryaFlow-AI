# KaryaFlow AI - Final Demo Video Script

Target length: **90 to 120 seconds**.

## Recording setup

- Use the live Render deployment: https://karyaflow-ai.onrender.com
- Use Chrome in a clean/private window.
- Hide bookmarks and unrelated tabs.
- Record at 1080p if possible.
- Do not narrate code; narrate the business decision.

## Timeline

### 0:00 - 0:10 | Problem + product

**Screen:** KaryaFlow landing/workspace.

**Voiceover:**
> Procurement teams validate the same transaction across a purchase order, invoice, and delivery challan. KaryaFlow turns those documents into an evidence-backed decision instead of another manual cross-check.

### 0:10 - 0:25 | One-click demo

**Action:** Click **Launch demo**.

**Voiceover:**
> I will use a realistic procurement transaction. KaryaFlow loads the three source documents and runs the verification workflow.

### 0:25 - 0:42 | Reconciliation

**Screen:** Verification result + checks.

**Voiceover:**
> Vendor, PO reference, and unit price match. Quantity does not: the PO authorizes 100 units, the invoice asks for 120, while delivery confirms 100.

### 0:42 - 0:58 | Evidence

**Action:** Click **Evidence** on Quantity.

**Voiceover:**
> The system does not ask the operator to trust a model. Every critical value is tied back to source evidence. Here we can compare the approved 100, invoiced 120, and delivered 100 side by side.

### 0:58 - 1:12 | Recommended action

**Action:** Click **Review action**.

**Voiceover:**
> KaryaFlow converts the discrepancy into an operational next step: ask the vendor to clarify the 20-unit variance.

### 1:12 - 1:22 | Human approval

**Action:** Approve the action.

**Voiceover:**
> The AI can prepare the action, but it cannot silently execute it. A human must explicitly approve the business step.

### 1:22 - 1:32 | Audit trail

**Action:** Open **Audit trail**.

**Voiceover:**
> The complete decision history is recorded: case creation, documents, analysis, action creation, and human approval.

### 1:32 - 1:40 | Close

**Screen:** Approved state / KaryaFlow logo.

**Voiceover:**
> KaryaFlow is not just document OCR or a chatbot. It is an evidence-first procurement workflow that moves from documents to a verified, auditable decision.

## Recording rules

- Keep the pointer visible but move it deliberately.
- Do not scroll unnecessarily.
- Pause briefly on the 100 / 120 / 100 evidence comparison.
- Do not show browser extensions, personal information, or unrelated windows.
- Do not claim capabilities that are not visible in the product.
